# Game Arcade: Learning Games for Primary School

A browser-based set of mini-games that borrows the feel of Minecraft and Roblox (blocks, building, rewards, streaks) to practise primary school skills: maths, spelling, vocabulary and science.

**Play link:** https://claude.ai/artifact/45CqzCVavFoKofoCMUoTcj

---

## 1. Goal

Give primary school children a game they actually want to play, while they quietly practise core skills. Every game rewards correct answers with sparkles, sounds, streaks and building progress, and wrong answers show the correct answer so the child learns from them.

## 2. Target users

- Children in primary school (about ages 6 to 12)
- Three difficulty levels: **Year 1-2**, **Year 3-4**, **Year 5-6**
- Parents and teachers who want a safe, ad-free practice tool

## 3. The games

### Block Quest
Mine a grid of blocks, answer a question to break each one, and collect blocks to build the Knowledge Castle.

- 20 blocks per world; a new world appears when all are mined
- Stone blocks = Maths, Wood blocks = Words, Diamond blocks = Science
- Players choose which subjects to mine
- Collect 4 blocks to unlock the next of 6 castle pieces (wall, gate, tower, windows, flag, crown)
- 3 hearts; a wrong answer costs a heart, and running out sends the player to camp to recover
- Streak counter (fire badge) for consecutive correct answers
- End screen shows accuracy and a badge: Master Builder, Expert Miner or Brave Explorer

### Memory Match
Flip cards to match each picture with its spelled word.

- 6, 8 or 10 pairs depending on level
- Tracks moves; fewer moves is better
- Skills: vocabulary, spelling recognition, memory

### Word Builder
Tap scrambled letters in the right order to spell the word shown by the picture.

- 5 words per round
- Undo button; a wrong try shakes the slots and resets the letters
- Score counts words spelled correctly on the first try
- Skills: spelling, letter-sound awareness

### Rocket Maths
Answer 10 maths questions in 45 seconds to fly the rocket to the moon.

- A wrong answer costs 3 seconds
- Questions scale by level (see below)
- Skills: mental maths, speed and accuracy

## 4. Learning content by level

| Level | Maths | Words | Science |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1-2 | Add and subtract within 10 | Short words (cat, apple, fish) | Animals, plants, colours |
| Year 3-4 | Times tables and division | Medium words (banana, rocket) | Earth, gases, mammals, freezing point |
| Year 5-6 | Bigger multiplication, fractions of numbers, perimeter | Longer words (volcano, astronaut) | Photosynthesis, gravity, planets, conductors |

## 5. Game feel and design

- Pixel-block visuals with textured stone, wood and diamond blocks
- Sky and grass background with drifting clouds (night sky in dark mode)
- Rounded, large, easy-to-read font (Fredoka)
- Hero picker (astronaut, superhero, wizard, cat, robot)
- Sound effects with a mute button
- Sparkle bursts, shake on wrong answers, pop animation for new castle pieces and confetti on winning

## 6. Technical notes

- Single self-contained HTML file; no installation needed
- Works on phones, tablets and computers
- Plain JavaScript; questions are generated in code (maths) or taken from small word banks (words and science)
- No accounts, no ads, and no data is collected or saved

## 7. Current limits

- This is a prototype. The word and science question banks are small and will repeat
- Progress is not saved between sessions
- Content is in English only
- Not yet tested across many devices

## 8. Ideas for next steps
